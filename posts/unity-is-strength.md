### Ai 翻译

collective sense of honor: strength and responsibility

the concept of collective honor has been a popular topic in various social settings, including schools. while some may question its effectiveness or even view it as a means of oppression, it cannot be denied that the power of collective honor is significant when harnessed properly.

unfortunately, there are still many educators who approach discipline and motivation from a capitalist perspective. punishing an entire group for one individual's mistake or demanding excessive repetition, are similar to how classic capitalists treated workers. such tactics merely shift class conflict between the boss and the workers to internal conflict among the workers themselves, and this unhealthy pattern should not be replicated in modern-day classrooms.

furthermore, teachers must stop scrutinizing students' grades, favoring those who excel and belittling others based on their performance. rather, they should strive to provide equal respect and opportunities for all students.

nonetheless, we understand that some young children who do not feel the warmth and support of teamwork may falsely perceive collective pride as detrimental. however, this fear can be avoided by offering examples of positive experiences wherein individuals have greatly benefited from collective action. one such example could involve inviting them to participate in group activities where older teammates show them kindness and guidance -helping them adapt and grow emotionally and intellectually.

in addition, though the idea of individualism might sometimes seem desirable, it cannot rival the strength and momentum of collective action towards a common goal. moreover, it needs to be crystal-clear that teamwork does not mean losing one's identity but actually enhancing it. collectivism instills a sense of belonging and values the qualities that make us unique, thus breaking down stereotypes and building a community of equals.

### 整体特征

大部分翻译的内容我都看了，整体来说，几乎每个翻译软件翻译过来都有一些不是很好。这不是说整篇文章不好，而是有一些**关键点**没有翻译好。

这些关键点完成可以是成为提升我们自己翻译的窍门。当然，在看的过程中，还查了不少的资料。

另外，这个翻译我认为整体比较好的是谷歌的，其次是 DeepL 的，还有就是百度的。Ai 没有翻译完整，是因为字数太多了，毕竟 2000 多字，所以不好评价。
